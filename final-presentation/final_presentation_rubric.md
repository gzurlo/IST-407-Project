# Rubric for the Final Presentation

The final presentation is where you present your **completed** project to the class on the last
day of class (**Dec 7**). Its goal is to tell the story of what you set out to do, what you did,
and what you found. This is a presentation only — the written narrative and code go in the
**final report** (due Dec 15; see `../final-report/`).

# Presentation

Your presentation will have 7–8 slides, as follows:

1. **Title** A title slide with the names of all team members. Please introduce yourselves during
   the presentation.
2. **Problem & Stakeholder** A succinct description of the domain problem and your primary
   stakeholder.
3. **Solution** What you set out to do, and how it connects to your stakeholder's need.
4. **Data** Tell us about the data you used — roughly what's in it and how much of it there is.
5. **Results (1 or 2 slides)** Your final results. Show the metrics that matter for your problem
   (e.g. F1 and a confusion matrix for classification; RMSE/MAE in original units for regression),
   not just accuracy. Include the visualizations or tables needed to support your claims.
6. **Limitations & Challenges** What limits your results, and the challenges you faced (and how you
   handled them).
7. **Conclusions & Future Work** What you conclude with respect to the stakeholder need, and where
   you would take the project next.

All team members must be present and should speak.

# Grading

- 1 point for each slide. −0.2 points for each slide that is not one of those requested above.

> This rubric is scored out of 7–8 and **scaled to the 5 project points** allocated to the Final
> Presentation in the syllabus.
